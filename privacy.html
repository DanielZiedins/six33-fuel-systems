SIX33 Fuel Systems is a pre-launch movement by Seek First World. This page explains exactly what this site collects and why. If anything here is unclear, email hello@thykingdom.net.

When you join the waitlist

  • Your email address, so we can tell you when the Kickstarter launches.
  • Your first name, only if you choose to give it.
  • A share code created for you, and the code of whoever invited you, if you arrived through someone's link. This is how we count who has brought people in.
  • Basic context about the signup: the page you came from, your screen size, your time zone, any campaign tags in the link, and how long the form took to fill in. We use these to understand which outreach works and to filter out bots.

The emails you'll get

One welcome email when you join, then launch news and updates about the mission. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect immediately. Emails are sent through Resend.

Anonymous page analytics

On the live site we count page views, which sections are reached, and which buttons are used. These counts contain no name, email, cookie or device identifier, and cannot be tied back to you. Add ?notrack to any address on this site to switch them off in your browser.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your email.
  • We don't use advertising trackers or third-party cookies.

Where it's stored

Waitlist records are stored in a Supabase database run by the Thy Kingdom Network. They are not publicly readable: the site can add a signup but cannot read the list back.

Removing your data

Email hello@thykingdom.net from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your record.
