Why We Put Scripture on Every Packet

Each SIX33 system carries its own verse. Here's why — and what Romans 12:11, Colossians 3:23, Psalm 23:3 and Psalm 4:8 have to do with energy, focus, recovery and rest.

Daniel ZiedinsFounder, SIX33 Fuel Systems

When we started designing SIX33, the first decision wasn't a flavour or a formula. It was this: every packet carries scripture. Not a logo that hints at faith. The actual words.

People sometimes ask whether that's a marketing angle. It's the opposite. If SIX33 were only a supplement brand, we wouldn't need the verses. They're there because the fuel is the doorway and the gospel is the point.

A packet is a small banner

Think about where a stick pack ends up: a gym bag, a desk drawer, a kitchen counter, a team's sideline cooler. Places where people don't usually see scripture, held by people who might not usually say it out loud.

In the same way, let your light shine before others, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven.

Matthew 5:16 (NIV)

A verse on a packet isn't a sermon. It's an opening. Someone asks, "What's 6:33?" and suddenly there's a conversation that wasn't going to happen otherwise.

Why each system has its own verse

We didn't pick four random verses. Each one matches what that system is for, so the moment you use it becomes a moment to remember something true.

Ignite — energy and focus. "Never be lacking in zeal, but keep your spiritual fervor, serving the Lord." — Romans 12:11 (NIV). Ignite is for the start: the early training session, the first push of the workday. Paul's point is that the fire we bring to our work should be fire for God, not just for the task.

Flow — productivity and deep work. "Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters." — Colossians 3:23 (NIV). Flow is for the long, focused hours. This verse turns ordinary work — studying, building, writing, coding — into something done for an audience of One.

Recover — recovery and electrolytes. "He restores my soul." — Psalm 23:3 (ESV). Recover is for after the effort. The verse reminds us that the deepest restoration isn't physical; it comes from the Shepherd who leads us beside still waters.

Rest — sleep and the nervous system. "In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, LORD, make me dwell in safety." — Psalm 4:8 (NIV). Rest is for the end of the day. David wrote this as a man with real enemies. Sleep, for him, was an act of trust.

Morning to night, one story

Put the four together and they trace a day: zeal in the morning, wholehearted work through the afternoon, restoration after the effort, peace at night. That's the shape of the SIX33 Fuel Map — and, we hope, the shape of a life lived on purpose.

What the verses are not

They're not a guarantee about the product, and they're not decoration. We won't use scripture to promise results it never promised. A verse about restoration doesn't mean a drink restores your soul — only God does that. The verse is there to point at Him.

The same idea, in other forms

SIX33 Fuel is one expression of a bigger brand family. You'll find the same conviction — faith worn openly, without apology — across SIX33 World and in the apparel at SIX33 Outpost.
