Why SIX33 Is Launching on Kickstarter

Why SIX33 Fuel Systems is launching through Kickstarter instead of a store, what founding backers get, and what we'll publish before the campaign goes live.

Daniel ZiedinsFounder, SIX33 Fuel Systems

SIX33 Fuel Systems isn't in a store yet. It's launching on Kickstarter first. Here's why we chose that route, what it means for the people who back it, and what we've committed to publishing before the campaign goes live.

Why not just open a shop?

A product like this needs a first production run before it exists in anyone's hands. That run has to be paid for up front. There are two ways to fund it: borrow money and hope people buy, or invite the people who believe in the mission to back it first.

We chose the second, for three reasons.

  • It's built with the people it's for. Founding backers aren't customers waiting on a delivery. They're the first people carrying the mission into their gyms, offices and churches.
  • It proves the idea honestly. If people don't want SIX33, a campaign tells us before we've made thousands of boxes. If they do, it tells us that too.
  • It keeps the mission first. Starting without heavy debt means decisions get made for the mission, not for a lender.

What backing means

Kickstarter isn't a shop, and it's worth being straight about that. When you back a campaign, you're supporting a project to be made, not buying something off a shelf. Rewards ship after the campaign succeeds and the product is produced.

There will be three backer tiers:

  • Scout — any one system, first run.
  • Operator — the full four-system box.
  • Vanguard — the founding circle, for people who want to be part of this from the very beginning.

Pricing for each tier will be revealed on launch day. Founding backers get the campaign's lowest pricing.

What we'll publish before launch

You shouldn't have to take a product on trust. Before the campaign goes live, we're committed to publishing the full ingredient panel for every system, so you can see exactly what you're backing and decide for yourself.

We'll also keep the language honest. SIX33 is fuel for people on mission. It isn't medicine, and we won't market it as if it were.

Building it the Kingdom way

SIX33 is being built by people who believe a business can be a vehicle for the gospel without compromising either. If that's a conversation you care about as a founder, Lions Den Alliance is an alliance of Kingdom businesses working through exactly those questions.

And SIX33 sits inside a bigger movement: Seek First World, built on the same verse.
